Alexander Nevsky Cathedral

The cathedral was built between 1882 and 1912 in memory of the soldiers who died in the Russo-Turkish War of 1877–78. That war led to Bulgaria's liberation after nearly five centuries of Ottoman rule.

It is one of the largest Eastern Orthodox cathedrals in Europe, and it is named after Saint Alexander Nevsky, a Russian prince and saint.

Step inside to see walls of Italian marble, onyx and alabaster, and murals by Russian and Bulgarian artists. The crypt holds a museum of Orthodox icons.
