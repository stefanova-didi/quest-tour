Sofia Old Town Quest

Welcome, The Explorers

Welcome to Sofia Old Town Quest! Over the next few hours your team will uncover eight landmarks hidden in riddles – from golden domes to Roman streets under your feet.

Solve each riddle, walk to the place it describes, take a team photo and learn its story. Wear comfortable shoes and keep your phone charged.

How it works

  • Tasks are played in order – solve one to unlock the next.
  • The clock starts when you tap Start and runs without pause. You have up to 4 hours.
  • Stuck? Hint 1 adds +10 min, hint 2 adds +15 min.
  • Giving up on a task shows the answer and adds +30 min.
  • Take a team photo at every landmark to unlock the next riddle.

About your photos. Photos you upload are collected and kept by your host. You won't see them in the app.

You'll confirm before the clock starts.
