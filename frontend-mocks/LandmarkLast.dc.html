Ancient Serdika Complex

Beneath the centre of modern Sofia lie the streets of Serdika, the Roman city that stood here almost 2,000 years ago.

The remains were uncovered while the metro was being built. Today you can walk among ancient streets, the foundations of an early Christian basilica and houses from the 4th to 6th centuries, right beside Serdika metro station.

Emperor Constantine the Great is said to have called the city "my Rome".
