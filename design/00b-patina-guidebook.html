Variant 00bLimestone & Patina · Guidebook

Quiet and bookish. One-pixel borders replace shadows, labels are small caps, the progress bar is ticked per task, and Literata takes over display duty from Fraunces.

Sofia reference. Same palette as 00, set like a printed city guide: hairline frames, small caps, a serif with full Cyrillic, tick-marked progress.

Components

1px hairline borders, 8px corners, outlined secondaries, no shadows, progress bar ticked into 8 tasks.

Fonts

Literata (serif, Cyrillic) display and italic team name; Atkinson body unchanged.

Highlights

Gold-100 panels with a gold-300 hairline and small-caps gold-700 labels; outlined gold tag in the header.

Icons

Thin outline, 1.5px stroke.
