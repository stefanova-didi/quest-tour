Quest City TourLook & feel proposals

Four green directions for the player app, each anchored in a real Sofia material or place, plus three component-and-type variants each of 00 Limestone & Patina and 02 Evening Domes. Every page shows the same five screens at 390 × 844 (Welcome, Task, hint confirmation, Landmark, Finish) so the directions compare fairly. Direction 00 is the current look.

Recommendation

★ 02c Evening Domes · Cards. Best match for the brief: every state (riddle, open hint, wrong answer) has its own container so it can be scanned while walking; the dark header makes the timer the highest-contrast element in sunlight; the segmented bar gives per-task feedback; filled icons hold up at small sizes; Onest and JetBrains Mono give full Cyrillic for a future Bulgarian UI; and it keeps the green-and-gold brand the app already has. Trim the card padding on long tourist texts when implementing.

★ 00c Limestone & Patina · Pills is the lowest-cost alternative: the shipped palette untouched, only radius and type tokens change, and Sofia Sans adds Bulgarian Cyrillic forms for free.

Whatever the choice: self-host the fonts, prefer filled or bold icons outdoors, and pick a display face with Cyrillic. The hairline variants (00b Guidebook, 02b Luxe) are the weakest in glare.

How to review

Open a direction, scroll the phone frames sideways, and judge three things the brief cares about: can you read the riddle in sunlight, is every penalty visible before it is paid (the hint sheet frame), and does the Finish screen feel like a reward. Then check the three notes under the frames: why it works, where it is weak, and what would have to change in the app.

Every direction is a token swap. The token names in themes/*.css match frontend-mocks/ds/questcity/tokens.json and frontend/src/styles/questcity.css, so adopting one means updating those values plus the font link in frontend/index.html. Two or more can also be mixed: for example Largo's header and numerals on the Mineral Baths palette.

All typefaces are self-hosted in fonts/ (SIL Open Font License), so the pages work offline and render the same on every machine. The same files can be copied into the app to replace the Google Fonts link.

To tweak a direction, edit its file in themes/ and reload. To change the sample screens for all directions, edit build.py and run python3 build.py.
